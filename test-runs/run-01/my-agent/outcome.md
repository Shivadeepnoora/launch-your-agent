# Rubric: this week's Tiny Tots Rhymes story packs

Grade each criterion pass/fail. All six must pass.

1. **3 complete packs.** `/mnt/session/outputs/pack-1/`, `pack-2/` and `pack-3/` each contain: `script.md` (2–4 minutes when narrated, with VISUAL scene notes for a bright 2D cartoon and NARRATION/LYRICS, including the full traditional rhyme), `metadata.md` (YouTube title of 70 characters or fewer, description, 10–15 tags, "Made for kids" note), `thumbnail-prompt.md` (a detailed image-generator prompt), and `thumbnail.png` (a 1280×720 image that opens and shows the short title).
2. **Public-domain rhymes only.** Every rhyme is a traditional public-domain nursery rhyme with traditional lyrics. No copyrighted characters, brands, modern songs, or another channel's original songs or arrangements.
3. **Toddler-appropriate.** Simple words, short sentences, a positive tone; nothing scary, violent or unsafe for a toddler to imitate. The content honestly fits YouTube's "made for kids" label.
4. **No repeats.** None of the 3 rhymes or story angles was already listed in the memory store's `used-rhymes.md` before this run, and `used-rhymes.md` has been updated with this week's 3 picks.
5. **Genuinely different.** Each pack has its own story angle, setting and lesson. They are not one template with names or rhymes swapped.
6. **Honest metadata.** Each title, description and thumbnail matches what actually happens in its script. No misleading clickbait.
